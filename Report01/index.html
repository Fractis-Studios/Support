Fractis Studios — Security Audit Report

Generated during investigation of a suspicious invoice email (INV160488).

Trigger

Suspicious invoice email (INV160488) received by Fractis Studios, purportedly from "Adam Ryder Brooker" sent from an obscure/tempmail address (ven200@libero.it), forwarding a fake invoice supposedly from finance@remit-payment.com (Cape Town, South Africa). Daleen forwarded the message to the investigator for review. The info@fractisstudios.com address appears only as the forwarding mailbox, not the original sender.

Email Forensics

  • Authentication: DKIM=pass, DMARC=pass, SPF=pass for fractisstudios.com.
  • Sending infrastructure: Authenticated sender info@fractisstudios.com via Microsoft 365 (DB4PR08MB9262.eurprd08.prod.outlook.com), relayed through Hostinger SMTP (148.222.54.35).
  • Implication: Pass results reflect the forwarding relay through Fractis infrastructure, not necessarily a credential compromise of info@.
  • Content: Classic "overdue invoice" phishing. Third party finance@remit-payment.com is an unknown remittance-style domain. No prior history with remit-payment.com or ven200@libero.it.

Website & DNS (Automated Check)

  • Site resolves and serves normally. HTTPS active. Cookie consent dialog present. Privacy/Terms/Cookie Policy linked in footer.
  • No obvious defacement or injected content on homepage.
  • DNS A: 88.222.223.48, 5.252.75.6. MX: mx1/mx2.hostinger.com. NS: ns1/ns2.dns-parking.com.
  • SPF: v=spf1 include:_spf.mail.hostinger.com ~all. DKIM (hostingermail1._domainkey): present. DMARC: v=DMARC1; p=none.
  • ⚠️ DMARC policy is p=none (monitor only). Recommend upgrading to p=quarantine then p=reject.
  • SPF uses ~all (soft fail). -all (hard fail) is stronger once mail flow confirmed.

Subdomain & Exposed-Path Scan (Automated)

  • Subdomains resolving: www (cdn.hstgr.net), ftp (153.92.11.148). No others. No dangling CNAMEs / takeover candidates.
  • Platform is WordPress. /wp-admin/ and /wp-login.php reachable (expected). /admin/ returns 200 — verify not unexpected panel.
  • Sensitive files (.env, .git/config, phpinfo, config.php, backups, SQL dumps) return 403/404 — not exposed.
  • security.txt not present (optional best practice).
  • No directory listing or exposed source detected.

Recommended Immediate Actions

  1. Reset passwords for info@ and any admin accounts; revoke active sessions/app passwords.
  2. Enable 2FA on mailbox and hosting panel.
  3. Audit mailbox rules & forwarding; remove auto-forwards.
  4. Check Sent Items for unauthorized outbound messages.
  5. Review Hostinger DNS & account activity logs.
  6. Scan endpoints of anyone with mailbox access for infostealer malware.
  7. Notify contacts the invoice email was not sent by Fractis Studios.
  8. Report finance@remit-payment.com / remit-payment.com as phishing.
  9. Keep WP core/plugins/themes updated; disable XML-RPC if unused; enforce 2FA on WP admin; install WAF; monitor wp-login brute force.
  10. Upgrade DMARC to quarantine/reject; consider SPF -all.

Socials

Audit each linked social account for unauthorized posts, changed bios/links, new admins, suspicious DMs, connected third-party apps. Revoke unknown OAuth apps and rotate passwords + enable 2FA.

Conclusion

Phishing originated externally via tempmail/obscure domain. No evidence of website defacement or data leak. Prioritize mailbox + hosting account hardening and DMARC upgrade.
